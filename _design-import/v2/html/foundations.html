01 · Brand vs Amber

Two reds that never collide

--c-brand is the logo color, lifted from the brand sheet. It belongs to the chrome — logo, splash, app icon, celebration moments, error accents. --c-amber stays exactly where it was: the Home tab accent, nothing more. They're never legal in the same role.

--c-brand · #ff3a14

The brand voice

The logo's red-orange. Reaches for it when the app is speaking as itself: chrome, celebration, error, urgency.

02 · Surface Trinity

Three surfaces, three jobs, no overlap

The single rule that fixes "everything is glass." Every component must reach into one of three semantic surfaces — never raw palette. --surface-hero is the one bold thing per screen. --surface-glass groups ambient content. --surface-flat is for rows, lists, dividers.

--surface-hero 1 per screen

The bold thing

Today's challenge. The verdict. The Start button. Solid fill, accent or photo.
Bgsolid · #1a1d26 or accent

Shadowdeep, grounded
--surface-glass grouping

Ambient grouping

A cluster of related content. Stat tiles, drill picker, settings group. Blurred & tinted.

05 · Cross-tab Reference

Source tab keeps the accent. Destination is text + arrow.

When tab A references tab B's content, the reference card stays in tab A's color. We never render a colored card from another tab inside this one — that's what makes the screen feel like a five-color rainbow.

06 · Signature easing

--ease-swish · the curve every celebration uses

cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.85, 0.3, 1) — fast in, slow settle. The motion equivalent of a ball dropping cleanly through the net. Every celebration moment uses this exact curve at five different durations.
